# 2309.07864 — The Rise and Potential of Large Language Model Based Agents: A Survey

> Source: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.07864> — Xi, Zhiheng, et al. (29 authors, Fudan NLP; 2023). PDF in `../../sources/arXiv/2309.07864-rise-of-llm-agents.pdf`.

## Thesis

A landmark early survey of the shift from a chat model to an *agent*: an LLM placed in a loop with perception and action, not just conversation. It frames an agent as three parts — a **brain** (the LLM, for reasoning and planning), **perception** (taking in the environment and other inputs), and **action** (using tools and acting in the world) — and maps the field across single agents, multi-agent systems, and human–agent cooperation, including the emergent behaviour of "agent societies." Written in 2023, it captures the moment the community reconceived the LLM from a text box into the core of an autonomous system.

## Relevance to the book

- **Ch1 §1.3 (From Chatbot to Agent)**: the academic grounding for the chapter's central arc — the model gaining "hands" and a loop, turning a chatbot into an agent. Supports the definition of an agent as brain + perception + action, and the move from single agents to multi-agent coordination.
