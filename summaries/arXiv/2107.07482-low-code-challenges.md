# Characteristics and Challenges of Low-Code Development: The Practitioners' Perspective

> Source: arXiv:2107.07482 (2021). Yajing Luo, Peng Liang, Chong Wang, Mojtaba Shahin, Jing Zhan. PDF in `../../sources/arXiv/2107.07482-low-code-challenges.pdf`.

## Thesis

Empirical study of low-code development (LCD) drawn from Stack Overflow and Reddit. LCD offers drag-and-drop GUIs and pre-built components so users with little or no programming expertise can build applications fast. But practitioners hold divergent views on its limits — maintainability, scalability, vendor lock-in, and suitability once needs outgrow the platform's templates.

## Relevance to the book

Ch3 §"Who Builds the Software": the low-code/no-code movement as a rung in the democratization arc, and its recurring lesson — hiding the code does not retire the engineering (maintainability/governance debt).
