# Summary — Establishment of Australian AI Safety Institute

> [!NOTE]
> **Source status:** Australian Government primary document — joint ministerial media release (Ministers
> Charlton & Ayres), 25 November 2025. Government announcement.

## Abstract

Announces the **Australian AI Safety Institute (AISI)** to evaluate and monitor AI-related risks and
harms, support regulators, and work with international partners; funded at ~$29.9M and set to begin
operations in early 2026. Part of the "keep Australians safe" pillar later folded into the National AI
Plan (2 Dec 2025).

## Key points

- Purpose: test/monitor emerging AI capabilities and harms; inform regulators and government.
- Funding ~$29.9M; operational early 2026; by mid-2026 testing unreleased frontier models.
- Sits within Australia's existing-law-first approach (no standalone AI Act).
