# Summary — National AI Plan: Empowering all Australians

> [!NOTE]
> **Source status:** Australian Government primary document — a ministerial media release (Ministers
> Ayres & Charlton, DISR portfolio), 2 December 2025, announcing the National AI Plan. Government
> policy statement, not independent analysis; read its "world-leading / first" claims as the
> government's own framing.

## Abstract

The Albanese Government's National AI Plan (2 Dec 2025) is a roadmap for an AI-enabled economy built on
three goals: capture opportunities (investment, infrastructure, local capability), spread the benefits
(public services, adoption, skills), and keep Australians safe. On regulation it marks a **change of
course**: rather than the standalone *mandatory guardrails* / possible AI Act floated in the 2024
proposals paper, Australia will rely on **existing laws** (privacy, consumer, copyright) and sector
regulators, supported by voluntary guidance and a new **Australian AI Safety Institute** (AISI, ~$29.9M,
operational early 2026) to test and monitor frontier models.

## Key points

- **Regulatory approach:** technology-neutral, existing-law-first; the 2024 mandatory-guardrails proposal
  is set aside as a standalone instrument.
- **AI Safety Institute:** established to evaluate/monitor emerging capabilities, risks and harms, and
  support regulators; by mid-2026 it was testing unreleased frontier models.
- **Investment/infrastructure:** positioning Australia to attract AI investment, including data-centre and
  compute infrastructure.
