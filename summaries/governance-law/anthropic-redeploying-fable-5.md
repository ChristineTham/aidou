# Summary — Anthropic, "Redeploying Claude Fable 5"

> [!NOTE]
> **Source status:** company primary statement (Anthropic), 30 June 2026. Interested party; core dates
> corroborated by reporting (CNBC, Al Jazeera).

## Abstract

On 30 June 2026 the US Commerce Department lifted the export controls on Fable 5 and Mythos 5. Fable 5
returned to users globally on 1 July 2026 after Anthropic shipped an improved safety classifier that
blocks the reported jailbreak "in over 99% of cases," developed with government partners including
Commerce's Center for AI Standards and Innovation. The general-release model had been offline ~19 days.

## Key points

- Controls lifted 30 June; Fable 5 restored worldwide 1 July; Mythos 5 access restored (reporting: ~26
  June for vetted US critical-infrastructure entities).
- Resolution was technical: a new classifier mitigating the specific exploit, plus government collaboration.
- Shows the suspension was temporary and negotiable once a mitigation existed.
