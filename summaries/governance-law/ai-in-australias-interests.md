# Summary — AI in Australia's interests

> [!NOTE]
> **Source status:** Australian Government primary document — a joint ministerial media release (PM
> Albanese, Ministers Ayres & Charlton), 15 July 2026, accompanying the PM's keynote of the same name.
> Government policy announcement; "world-leading / first country" claims are the government's own.

## Abstract

On 15 July 2026 the government set out the next step from the National AI Plan: it will **legislate a
single set of national AI Standards** — described as the first framework to bring AI's economic, social,
national-security and environmental dimensions together — and has created an **Office of AI** inside the
Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet to design them and coordinate a whole-of-government approach.
The most concrete new rules target **data centres**, building on the government's Data Centre Expectations.

## Key points

- **Office of AI:** new, within PM&C; coordinates the design of the Australian AI Standards across
  portfolios (working with Ministers Ayres and Charlton).
- **Legislated AI Standards:** a single national framework (economic, social, security, environmental),
  positioned as world-first.
- **Data-centre obligations (the "data infrastructure guardrails"):** large centres to underwrite their
  own new power supply, pay their full share of grid-connection costs (so household bills are not
  affected), curtail power when the grid is strained, and be as water-efficient as possible.
- **Creative works:** commitment that AI training must not use Australian creative works without the
  artist's consent/control.
