# ISO/IEC 42005:2025 — AI system impact assessment

> [!NOTE]
> Source: [iso-42005.md](../../sources/ai-governance/iso-42005.md) — ISO/IEC, *Information technology — Artificial intelligence — AI system impact assessment* (ISO/IEC 42005:2025), International Organization for Standardization, May 2025. https://www.iso.org/standard/42005.html
> Metadata verified via the ISO catalogue and SC 42 records; the standard text itself is paywalled, so this summary rests on the published catalogue scope, not the full clauses.

## Abstract

ISO/IEC 42005:2025 is the first-edition international standard giving organisations guidance on how and when to perform an AI system impact assessment — a structured evaluation of an AI system's potential effects on individuals, groups, and societies across its life cycle — and how to document it. It was prepared by ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 (Artificial intelligence), the same subcommittee behind the terminology (22989), risk-management (23894), and management-system (42001) standards the book already cites.

## Where it fits

It is the impact-assessment member of the SC 42 family: 22989 supplies the vocabulary, 23894 the risk process, 42001 the certifiable management system, and 42005 the method for assessing impact and relating it to the other governance processes. Where the EU AI Act requires a fundamental-rights impact assessment for certain high-risk systems, 42005 is a ready method an organisation can follow rather than invent.
