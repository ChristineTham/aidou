## Abstract

Palo Alto Networks' *2026 Identity Security Landscape* is a vendor survey of 2,930 global cybersecurity leaders, reporting on how enterprises are securing human and machine identities in an AI-agent era. Its headline claim, as it appears verbatim in the extraction, is: "Machine identities, including AI agents, now outnumber human identities 109:1." The report frames this as a "species shift" away from a human-only workforce, and projects that "AI agents are expected to grow by 85%" this year. It is a marketing-adjacent document launched alongside a product, so the figures are self-reported and unaudited. For this book it supports only one point: the year-on-year rise in the machine-to-human identity ratio (from 82:1 in the prior CyberArk landscape to 109:1 here).

- **No figure on the share of machine identities that are AI agents** appears in the extraction beyond the "85%" growth projection.
- **No figure on the proportion of organisations running autonomous agents in production** appears in the extraction.
- The extraction does not itself state the prior-year **82:1** ratio; the 82:1 → 109:1 trajectory is established by pairing this source with the earlier CyberArk landscape, not from this page alone.
