# ISO/IEC 42006:2025 — Requirements for bodies auditing and certifying AI management systems

> [!NOTE]
> Source: [iso-42006.md](../../sources/ai-governance/iso-42006.md) — ISO/IEC, *Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Requirements for bodies providing audit and certification of artificial intelligence management systems* (ISO/IEC 42006:2025), International Organization for Standardization, July 2025. https://www.iso.org/standard/42006.html
> Metadata verified via the ISO catalogue (first edition; stage 60.60 reached 2025-07-07; ~31 pages); the standard text is paywalled, so this rests on the catalogue scope.

## Abstract

ISO/IEC 42006:2025 specifies requirements, and gives guidance, for the bodies that audit and certify AI management systems (AIMS) against ISO/IEC 42001. It supplements the general certification-body standard ISO/IEC 17021-1 with AI-specific competence and process requirements — the machinery that makes a 42001 certificate mean something, by governing the certifiers themselves.

## Where it fits

Without a standard for the auditors, "certified to 42001" would carry no consistent assurance; 42006 sets the bar certification bodies must clear to demonstrate they can competently assess an organisation's AI management system. It is the credibility layer beneath 42001 certification, and part of what makes certification a usable way to demonstrate conformity to regulators.
