## Abstract

The *2025 Identity Security Landscape* is CyberArk's annual international survey study, published 23 April 2025, arguing that the rapid enterprise adoption of AI and cloud has "inadvertently created a new identity-centric attack surface" built on unmanaged, unsecured machine identities. Its headline claim, stated verbatim, is that "There are 82 machine identities for every human in organizations worldwide" (an 82:1 ratio), and that "AI will be the #1 creator of new identities with privileged and sensitive access in 2025." The report says machine identities — driven primarily by cloud and AI — vastly outnumber humans, that 42% of machine identities have privileged or sensitive access, yet 88% of organisations define a "privileged user" as applying solely to human identities. It frames identity silos (root cause of cyber risk for 70% of respondents), hybrid infrastructure and shadow AI as the primary drivers of a widening, poorly-visible attack surface, and positions the coming privileged access of AI agents as "an entirely new threat vector."

## Methodology / About the report

- Conducted by market researchers **Vanson Bourne**.
- **2,600 cybersecurity decision-makers** surveyed.
- Organisations of **500 employees and above**, private and public sector.
- **20 countries**: Brazil, Canada, Mexico, US, France, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands, Spain, UK, UAE, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, Australia, India, Hong Kong, Israel, Japan, Singapore and Taiwan.
- Published/announced **April 23, 2025** (dateline: Newton, Mass. & Petach Tikva, Israel).

## Headline findings (verbatim / near-verbatim)

- **"There are 82 machine identities for every human in organizations worldwide."** (rendered as **82:1** on the landing page: "Machine identities outnumber human identities").
- **"AI will be the #1 creator of new identities with privileged and sensitive access in 2025"** (landing page "#1"); the press release phrases it: "AI is expected to drive the creation of the greatest number of new identities with privileged and sensitive access in 2025."
- **70%** say identity silos are a root cause of organisational cybersecurity risk.

## Machine-identity growth and privilege sprawl ("Rise of the machines")

Machine identities, driven primarily by cloud and AI, now vastly outnumber human identities, and "nearly half" have sensitive or privileged access:

- **88%** of respondents say the definition of a "privileged user" in their organisation applies **solely to human identities** — yet **42%** of machine identities have privileged or sensitive access.
- **61%** do not have identity security controls in place to secure cloud infrastructure and workloads.
- **87%** say their organisation experienced **at least two successful identity-centric breaches** in the past 12 months (supply chain attacks, compromised privileged access, identity and credential theft).
- Human and machine identities — many with privileged access — are **expected to double in 2025**.

## AI and agentic-AI identity risk

- **AI is expected to drive the creation of the greatest number of new identities with privileged and sensitive access in 2025** (the "#1" headline).
- Only **32%** of organisations have the right identity security controls despite big AI spending (landing page: "Organizations are spending big money on AI—but only 32% have the right identity security controls").
- **68%** say their organisations **lack identity security controls for AI**.
- **47%** **cannot secure shadow AI usage** in their organisation.
- AI agent adoption roadblocks include **manipulation and sensitive access concerns** (external manipulation and privileged access of AI agents).
- Press release framing: "the privileged access of AI agents will represent an entirely new threat vector" (Clarence Hinton, Chief Strategy Officer, CyberArk).

## Complexity, identity silos and resilience

- **70%** say identity silos are a **root cause** of organisational cybersecurity risk.
- **75%** of security professionals agree their organisations **prioritise business efficiencies over robust cybersecurity**.
- **88%** are under **increased pressure from insurers** mandating enhanced privilege controls.
- Primary causes of risk-generating identity silos are **hybrid infrastructures and shadow AI**.

## Attributed positioning quote

> "The race to embed AI into environments has inadvertently created a new set of identity security risks centered around the access of unmanaged and unsecured machine identities – and the privileged access of AI agents will represent an entirely new threat vector." — Clarence Hinton, Chief Strategy Officer at CyberArk.
