*The Way of AI, grounded in practice (道)*

**愛 (ai)** — love: care for the people the work touches.
**道 (dō)** — the way: a craft refined over a lifetime.

*AI-dō* joins the two — the way of AI guided by love. Say it aloud and it rhymes with *Play-Doh*, and that is the right instinct: hold the tool loosely, like clay, shaping and reshaping one push at a time. Read it as a path, not a manual.
