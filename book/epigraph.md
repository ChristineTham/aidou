*The Way of AI, grounded in practice*

- **愛 (ai)** — Japanese word for love: care for the people the work touches.
- **道 (dō)** — *the way, path, or disciplined practice*: a craft refined over a lifetime, as in `judō`.

*AI道* represents *a disciplined way of working with AI, guided by human-centred principles*.

It rhymes with *Play-Doh*, and that is intentional: use AI like clay, shaping and reshaping one push at a time. Read this book as a path, not a manual.
