# Computer Science Achievement and Writing Skills Predict Vibe Coding Proficiency

> Source: arXiv:2603.14133 (14 Mar 2026). Sverrir Thorgeirsson, Theo B. Weidmann, Zhendong Su (ETH Zürich). PDF in `pdf/2603.14133.pdf`.

## Thesis

Preregistered study, 100 tertiary students, expert-curated tasks in a purpose-built environment mirroring commercial tools. **Both writing skill and CS achievement significantly predict vibe-coding performance**; CS knowledge stays predictive even after controlling for general cognitive ability. Strong writing does **not** fully compensate for weak CS fundamentals.

## Relevance to the book

The nuance that keeps the Ch3 claim honest: the best AI-software builder combines domain/intent articulation (writing) with enough software understanding (CS) — matching the author's framing of a subject-matter expert who *also* understands software engineering, not a pure non-coder.
