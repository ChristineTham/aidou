# Professional Software Developers Don't Vibe, They Control: AI Agent Use for Coding in 2025

> Source: arXiv:2512.14012 (16 Dec 2025). Ruanqianqian Huang, Avery Reyna, Sorin Lerner, Haijun Xia, Brian Hempel (UC San Diego). PDF in `pdf/2512.14012.pdf`.

## Thesis

Field observations (N=13) + survey (N=99) of experienced developers using AI agents. They value agents as productivity boosts but **retain agency over design and implementation**, insisting on fundamental quality attributes. Rather than passively accepting suggestions, they use deliberate strategies to steer the agent, leaning on domain and engineering expertise to complement its limits and keep control of critical architectural decisions.

## Relevance to the book

Counterweight for Ch3: expertise still matters. The shift is from typing code to *steering* — control through judgement, not abdication — which is exactly the ICE "stay in the loop" discipline.
