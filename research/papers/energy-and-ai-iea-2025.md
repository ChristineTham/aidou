# energy-and-ai-iea-2025 — Energy and AI (World Energy Outlook Special Report)

> Source: https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai — International Energy Agency (IEA) (2025).

## Thesis

The IEA's flagship special report quantifies the energy demand of data centres and AI. It finds data centres consumed roughly 415 TWh in 2024 (about 1.5% of global electricity), growing about 12% per year over the prior five years, and projects consumption to roughly double to about 945 TWh by 2030 in its Base Case — just under 3% of global electricity. The growth is disproportionately AI: accelerated (AI) servers are projected to grow about 30% per year versus 9% for conventional servers, accounting for almost half of the net increase. The lesson: AI's energy footprint is real and rising fast, concentrated in a few regions (US ~+240 TWh, China ~+175 TWh by 2030), even though data centres remain a small single-digit share of total global demand.

## Relevance to the book

Anchors the Chapter 5 subsection on the environmental / sustainability cost of AI. It provides the authoritative macro figures — the "how big is this" numbers from a named, credible institution (IEA) rather than advocacy estimates — against which per-prompt figures can be contextualized. It also supplies the honest counterweight the book needs: data centres are still ~1.5% of global electricity today, so the story is one of rapid growth and regional grid stress, not (yet) a dominant share of world energy.
