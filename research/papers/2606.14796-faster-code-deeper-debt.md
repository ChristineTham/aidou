# Faster Code, Deeper Debt? A Multivocal Literature Review on Technical Debt and Its Early Signs in LLM-Assisted Software Development

> Source: arXiv:2606.14796 (2026). Ramtin Ehsani, Shriya Rawal, Yuanfang Cai, Preetha Chatterjee (Drexel University). PDF in `pdf/2606.14796.pdf`.

## Thesis

Multivocal review of 104 sources on technical debt in LLM-assisted development. Finding: LLMs **amplify the traditional debts** — code, design, documentation — and add novel ones (fast-integration debt, prompt debt, governance debt, ethical debt, data debt). Recommends human-in-the-loop frameworks; flags the lack of standardised metrics for LLM-specific debt.

## Relevance to the book

Ch3 §"Who Builds the Software" balance, and §"Quality over Slop": faster generation can mean deeper debt, so software-engineering discipline (structure, tests, maintenance, review) is what separates leverage from unmaintainable slop.
