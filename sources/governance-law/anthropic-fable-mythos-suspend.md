# Statement on the US government directive to suspend access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5

- Source URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access
- Author: Anthropic (company statement)
- Date: 12 June 2026
- Type: Company statement (primary source).

## Extracted facts

- On 12 June 2026 (received "today at 5:21pm ET"), the US government, citing national-security
  authorities, issued an **export-control directive** to suspend all access to **Fable 5** and
  **Mythos 5** by any foreign national — whether inside or outside the US, including foreign-national
  Anthropic employees.
- To comply, Anthropic **disabled both models for all customers** (worldwide). Access to all other
  Anthropic models was unaffected.
- **Mythos 5** is Anthropic's most capable model, previewed early April 2026 and kept tightly
  restricted for its exceptional ability to find software security vulnerabilities. **Fable 5**
  (released ~9 June 2026) is a guardrailed, general-release version of Mythos 5.
- **Stated reason:** the government believed it had become aware of a method of bypassing
  ("jailbreaking") Fable 5. Anthropic reviewed the demonstration — described as asking the model to
  read a codebase and identify software flaws, a capability widely available in competing models —
  and **disagreed** that a narrow potential jailbreak justified recalling a commercial model
  deployed to hundreds of millions of people; applying such a standard across the industry would
  "essentially halt all new model deployments for all frontier model providers."
- Reporting (WSJ) attributed the trigger to concerns raised by Amazon CEO Andy Jassy to the White House.
