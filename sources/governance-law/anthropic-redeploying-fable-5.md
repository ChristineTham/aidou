# Redeploying Claude Fable 5

- Source URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5
- Author: Anthropic (company statement)
- Date: 30 June 2026
- Type: Company statement (primary source).

## Extracted facts

- "As of today, June 30, the export controls on Fable 5 and Mythos 5 have been lifted."
- **Fable 5** restored to users globally on **1 July 2026** (Claude Platform, Claude.ai, Claude Code,
  Claude Cowork). **Mythos 5** access also restored (per Anthropic's update; reporting notes Mythos 5
  returned ~26 June for a set of vetted US critical-infrastructure entities).
- The controls had been imposed on 12 June 2026 after a reported method to bypass Fable 5's
  safeguards for identifying software vulnerabilities.
- **Fix:** Anthropic shipped an improved safety classifier — the specific technique in the Amazon
  report is now blocked "in over 99% of cases" — developed with government partners including the
  Department of Commerce's Center for AI Standards and Innovation (CAISI).
- Net effect: a shutdown of roughly 19 days for the general-release model.
