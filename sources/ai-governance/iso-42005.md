# ISO/IEC 42005:2025 — AI system impact assessment

Source (catalogue page, JS-gated; metadata verified via ISO catalogue and SC 42 records): https://www.iso.org/standard/42005.html
Standard: ISO/IEC 42005:2025, "Information technology — Artificial intelligence — AI system impact assessment"
Edition: 1 (first edition), published May 2025
Committee: ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 (Artificial intelligence)

## Scope

ISO/IEC 42005:2025 gives guidance for organisations performing AI system impact assessments — how and when to assess the potential impacts of an AI system on individuals, groups of individuals, and societies, across the AI system life cycle. It includes considerations for how to document such assessments and how an impact assessment relates to other AI governance and risk-management processes (notably an AI management system per ISO/IEC 42001 and AI risk management per ISO/IEC 23894).

It is the impact-assessment member of the SC 42 family, sitting alongside the terminology standard (22989), the risk-management guidance (23894), and the certifiable management system (42001). Where the EU AI Act requires a fundamental-rights impact assessment for certain high-risk systems, 42005 supplies a method organisations can follow.
