# ISO/IEC 42006:2025 — Requirements for bodies auditing and certifying AI management systems

Source (catalogue page, JS-gated; metadata verified via ISO catalogue and SC 42 records): https://www.iso.org/standard/42006.html
Standard: ISO/IEC 42006:2025, "Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Requirements for bodies providing audit and certification of artificial intelligence management systems"
Edition: 1 (first edition), published July 2025 (stage 60.60 reached 2025-07-07); ~31 pages
Committee: ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 (Artificial intelligence)

## Scope

ISO/IEC 42006:2025 specifies requirements and provides guidance for bodies that audit and certify AI management systems (AIMS) against ISO/IEC 42001. It supplements the general certification-body requirements of ISO/IEC 17021-1 with AI-specific competence and process requirements — the machinery that makes a 42001 certificate credible, by governing the certifiers themselves. Without it, "certified to 42001" would carry no assurance of a consistent bar; with it, certification bodies must demonstrate the competence to assess an organisation's AI management system.
